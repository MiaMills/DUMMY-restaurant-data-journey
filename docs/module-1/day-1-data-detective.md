## What I learned

Write 2 - 4 sentences in your own words about what became clearer to you during this exercise.

This task reminded me of what data is and helped me to distinguish between the three main types of data: 
**quantitative data**, which is **measured or counted**, such as the number of orders received each day; 
**qualitative data**, which is **descriptive** and may include an **opinion**, such as a customer review; and an
**identifier**, such as the order ID **BAO125**, which identifies a particular order. 

It also improved my understanding of how data can be further classified: 
**structured data** is organised into fixed **fields, rows and columns**, such as the restaurant transaction table; 

**semi-structured data** uses **keys or labels**, for example JSON such as `"order_id": "BAO125", "item": "Baobab drink", "quantity": 2`; and 

**unstructured data** has no fixed format, such as a photograph of a meal or an audio recording of a customer-support call. I also became more confident in identifying these features within a data set and using them to classify data accurately.
