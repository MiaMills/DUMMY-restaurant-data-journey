# Module 1 Day 1: Business Question to Data Question

## Exercise purpose

This exercise helped me turn a broad business question into a question that can be answered with data.

## My questions

### Question 
**Business question:**  
A business question is a broader question about performance, decisions or actions in the business. It focuses on what the business wants to understand or improve.
An example of a business question is: 
1. Which pizzas should Baobab and Sorrel promote to increase profit? or
2. At which times should Baobab and Sorrel run more pizza promotions?

**Data question:**  
A specific question that can be answered by collecting, measuring and analysing data to identify patterns, trends or relationships and support informed decision-making.

An example of a data question is: 
Which pizzas generate the highest profit based on their selling prices, ingredient costs and quantities sold?
Which days of the week and times of day have the lowest pizza sales, based on the number of orders and total sales revenue?

**Fields or data needed:**  
A field is a single category of information stored about each record in a dataset. For instance: 
The following fields are required for this business question: Which pizzas generate the highest profit based on their selling prices, ingredient costs and quantities sold?
**Fields or data needed:**

- Pizza ID
- Pizza name/type
- Selling price (£)
- Ingredient cost per pizza (£)
- Quantity sold
- Total sales revenue (£)
- Total ingredient costs (£)
- Profit per pizza (£)
- Total profit per pizza type (£)

The following fields are required for this business question: Which days of the week and times of day have the lowest pizza sales, based on the number of orders and total sales revenue?
- Order ID
- order date
- day of the week
- order time
- pizza ID 
- pizza name/type
- quantity sold 
- selling price (£) 
- total sales revenue (£)

**Can the current dataset answer it?**  
For the first data question: Which pizzas generate the highest profit based on their selling prices, ingredient costs and quantities sold:
The dataset contains pizza names, prices and sales records, allowing sales revenue and quantities sold to be calculated. 
However, it does not include ingredient costs, so actual profit cannot be determined.

For the second data question: Which days of the week and times of day have the lowest pizza sales, based on the number of orders and total sales revenue:
The dataset contains order IDs, dates, times and pizza prices. These fields allow the business to calculate sales revenue, count orders 
and identify the days and times with the lowest sales.

## What I learned
I learned that a business question focuses on what a company wants to achieve or improve, whereas a data question focuses on what can be measured and analysed 
to support the company in achieving its goals. Data questions help businesses make informed decisions based on evidence rather than assumptions. However, I also 
learned that datasets have limitations, as they may not contain all the information needed to answer certain questions fully. Therefore, it is important to identify
the relevant data fields, assess whether the available data is sufficient and recognise when additional data needs to be collected.
