Day 1 Reflection

One thing I understand more clearly about data now is:
I now have a better understanding of the three categories of data organisation: structured, unstructured and semi-structured data. I have also learned about the terminology used to describe different data formats, such as JPEG, CSV and text files. Furthermore, I recognise the importance of the relationship between business questions and data questions, and how data analysis can support evidence-based business decisions.

One example of quantitative data and one example of qualitative data is:
An example of quantitative data is the price of a menu item, as it represents a measurable numerical value. An example of qualitative data is a photograph of a meal served to a customer, as it provides visual information about the presentation and appearance of the food.

One question I would like to answer using restaurant data is:
How can data collection methods be improved to minimise gaps and limitations in the available data, ensuring that it can more effectively answer relevant business and data questions?

One concept I want to practise further is:
I would like to develop my ability to translate broad business questions into specific, measurable data questions more efficiently. As an aspiring data scientist, I also want to strengthen my ability to critically and objectively evaluate datasets, identifying their strengths, limitations and opportunities for further analysis. This will help me determine whether the available data is sufficient to answer a question or whether additional information needs to be collected.
